HOW TO PLAY

FROGGER is a time-based game.  
Use the ARROW KEYS to move the frog across the river and reach 
the burrows before time runs out!  
If time runs out and you have not reached a burrow, 
you will lose a life.
The river is full of crocodiles, which can shoot projectiles.
Press ENTER to make the frog shoot two granades both left and 
right to neutralize any incoming crocodile bullet.


SCORING:

- Reach a burrow: +500 points
- Time bonus: +20 per second left
- Move forward (KEY_UP): +9 points
- Move down (KEY_DOWN): -5 points
- Complete all burrows without dying: +1500 points
